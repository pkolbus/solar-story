Corrections and Amplifications
------------------------------

* I significantly underreported the number of Home Assistant users. As of summer 2026, the user base is estimated at around 2.5 million. The number shared in the presentation was the count that are providing analytics data.
* Roof area covered by my solar shingles is approximately 800 square feet, or 74.5 square meters. Each 70W shingle is approximately 16.9" x 46.8", or about 5.5 square feet of roof area.  
* Compatibility with Eve was asked during the questions. Not only is this brand supported, they are a member of the "Works with Home Assistant" partner program: <https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/eve/>.
* For compatibility in general:
    1. Start with the [Integrations]<https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/> list for the list that are supported out of the box.
    2. The [Home Assistant Community Store (HACS)](https://hacs.xyz/) is an excellent resource for community-developed integrations.
    3. Many integration developers share their projects on the [Home Assistant Forums](https://community.home-assistant.io/). The forums are also a great place to browse for automation ideas and get help.
